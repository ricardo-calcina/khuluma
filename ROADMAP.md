# Direction

Khuluma, the menu-bar app, is the product. The CLI remains its back end while native replacements are developed and verified. Current tasks, priorities and decisions live in [`LOG.md`](LOG.md).

Dictation stays local and fast, recovery must not lose a take, and usage/logs hold counts and timings. The Mac App Store is parked. The earlier detailed rename and CLI-sunset roadmap is [archived](docs/archive/KHULUMA_RENAME_ROADMAP.md); it records proposals and research, not completed distribution work.
